============================================================================================================

0.30 / notional. The cost therefore falls hyperbolically as the coin rises and rises without

============================================================================================================
HONEST LIMITS
============================================================================================================
1. EVERY FEE HERE IS THE FLOOR. The 0.02%/side rate is published as a minimum and the volume
   ladder above it is UNSOURCED after four attempts. Nothing was invented to fill it, so every
   number above is the cheapest the account can be, not the likeliest.
2. THE SPREAD IS A SNAPSHOT, NOT A CLOCK. Seven polls twelve seconds apart. R480 showed these
   books move by hours of the day and that Coinbase's empties for one hour daily; none of that
   is in these figures.
3. MARKS RE-PRICE. Every notional, fee and crossing above is true for this run's snapshot only.
   That is the finding, not a caveat: the whole point is that the cost moves with the price.
4. THE CROSSING FREQUENCIES ARE A CRYPTO BASE RATE, NOT A FORECAST. They say how often a
   coin-day in 2020-2026 was followed by a move that far. They are pooled across eleven coins,
   so a specific contract's own volatility is not in them, and they are not applied to the
   non-crypto contracts at all.
5. US 500 / TECH WERE SHUT AT POLL TIME, so their spread and therefore their all-in cost is
   UNMEASURED, not measured-and-wide. The fee half is sourced and stands. Re-polling them inside
   their own session is a free follow-up and is queued as such - and their SPY/QQQ pairing would
   still be a proxy, not the thing.
6. NOTHING HERE SAYS AN INSTRUMENT HAS AN EDGE. A cheap round trip is a cost fact. Whether the
   method works on gold or an index perpetual is a question this round did not and could not ask.
