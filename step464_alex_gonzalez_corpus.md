## 3. "SET AND FORGET" — the actual mechanics

### 3a. The whole setup, in his own summary

> "You're **trading with the trend**. You're buying or selling at a **strong

> "everything of that trade that I took was based off of **trend**, was based

> and then my **entry signal**."
> — `grw58BIzotU.txt` 08:15:30, **2025-09-28**

### 3b. Entry trigger — break and retest, confirmed by a body close

> "You can only take the trade **once you've had the break and then the
> retest**. … 'Hey, Alex, can I enter the trade at the break at the high?' Yes,
> you could. You could enter the trade at the break. Just understand, you cannot
> add that as a break and retest confluence."
> — `grw58BIzotU.txt` 06:11:14, **2025-09-28**

> "a break and retest is a **continuation pattern** … personally, it's one of my
> favorite continuation patterns."
> — `grw58BIzotU.txt` 06:18:11, **2025-09-28**

Structure shifts are confirmed on the **body close only**, not the wick:

> "you only confirm that this market is bearish once we have **body candlestick
> closed below** … It is only confirmed shifting structure once this candlestick
> has closed below."
> — `grw58BIzotU.txt` 04:02:48, **2025-09-28**

His reversal pattern is head-and-shoulders, taken at the neckline, not the
shoulder:

> "Selling at the right shoulder is extremely high risk. I don't recommend it
> unless you are an experienced trader. **Selling at the break and retest of the
> neckline** of the head and shoulders is where it is the proper reversal trade
> confirmation because you have the confirmed shift of structure."
> — `grw58BIzotU.txt` 06:54:46, **2025-09-28**

Final trigger candle is an engulfing candle on the entry timeframe:

> "and then your **entry signal, which is the bearish engulfing candlestick on
> the 1 hour**."
> — `grw58BIzotU.txt` 07:37:58, **2025-09-28**

The only indicator, and he explicitly demotes it:

> "there's only one that sticks, and that's the **50 EMA**."
> — `grw58BIzotU.txt` 07:21:06, **2025-09-28**

> "The EMA is nothing but a delayed support and resistance. … it has **zero
> importance** when it comes to trading. You're not going to build a whole

> — `grw58BIzotU.txt` 07:26:35, **2025-09-28**

### The specific thing this corpus answers for the bot

The brief framed it as "TJR's answer is add London, Alex's is stop being
session-bound at all." **That framing is wrong and the corpus says so.** Alex is
*more* session-bound on entries than expected — he has a 01:00–10:30 window, a
Monday–Wednesday preference and a Thursday-morning hard stop. What he does not
have is a **time-based exit**.

So the two men do not disagree about when to *enter*. They disagree about
whether a clock is allowed to *close* a trade. If the 31 of 51 missed days are
attributable to the 10:30 cutoff, Alex's material does not obviously rescue
them, because he would not have entered after 10:30 either. The lever his method
actually offers is the other one: letting a position live past the close.
