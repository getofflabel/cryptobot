1. DOES A HOLD ACTUALLY STRADDLE A SETTLEMENT?
========================================================================================================
THE QUEUE ITEM SAYS THE METHOD 'HOLDS 24 HOURS'. IT DOES NOT.
24 hours is the CAP. The stop is the sweep-to-entry extreme and it
is tight, so most positions are gone long before a settlement.

3. WHERE THE SIGN COMES FROM - LONG vs SHORT
========================================================================================================
Funding is not symmetric in crypto: the historical average rate is
positive, so longs pay and shorts collect. The method takes both,
so the NET depends entirely on its own long/short mix.
