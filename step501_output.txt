============================================================================================================
WHAT THIS ROUND ESTABLISHES
============================================================================================================

  1. THE COVERAGE COLUMN EXISTS and it is not R494's. Behind their own fences the eleven
     instruments run 17.8% to 90.6% full. PAXG's fenced coverage is 17.8%, not the 10.6%
     the published caveat carries.

  2. THINNESS ALONE IS NOT THE BIAS. Uniform random thinning from 80% down to 5% full moves
     the coordinate 0.892x - 1.001x on 6 donors. The queue item's framing - that a ranking whose
     numerator is biased 1.0x to 1.3x row by row is not a ranking - is right about the
     consequence and wrong about the cause: coverage does not predict the bias, GAP SHAPE does.

  3. EVERY ROW'S GAP SHAPE INFLATES ITS COORDINATE, AND BY SIMILAR AMOUNTS: 1.036x to 1.144x
     across the 10 rows that can be measured, median 1.071x - including BTC at 1.067x and ETH at
     1.053x, the two densest tapes on the disk. The bias is real, it is NOT confined to the sparse
     rows, and because it is COMMON to the rows it mostly cancels in a ranking. That is why the
     ordering barely moves, and it is a different reason from the one the queue item expected.

  4. THE ORDERING: 44 of 45 pairwise orderings survive, 1 reverses.
     The one that does not: XRPUSD over SOLUSD. Both are INTACT/SPENT rows of a ranking used to
     decide where a sealed look goes, so the reversal is the round's operational output.

  5. 0 row(s) UNREADABLE, 1 row(s) NOT MEASURABLE, 1 row(s) SPLIT between the two
     estimators. Those are three different states and the table above keeps them apart.

  FENCE HELD: simulate() never called, no entry population built, no sweep scanned, no stop
  measured, no outcome read, every tape read behind its own 80% boundary. NO LOOK CONSUMED.
