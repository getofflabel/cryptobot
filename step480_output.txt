  MECHANISM, PRIMARY-SOURCED (docs.cdp.coinbase.com/derivatives/introduction/
  market-hours, read 2026-08-12): Coinbase Derivatives runs 24x7 for 24x7-enabled
  products, halting only Fridays 16:00-16:50 CT. But NON-24x7 PARTICIPANTS take
  'a one-hour break each day from 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM CT'. That is 21:00-22:00 UTC,
  exactly the hour measured. The market stays OPEN; a large share of who quotes it
  goes home. 2026-08-11 was a TUESDAY, so the weekly Friday halt cannot explain it.

  WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN A COST LINE: the method holds 24 hours, so EVERY
  position spans this hour. If a stop triggers inside it, the book that has to
  absorb the exit is the thin one. R479's headline - Coinbase supports ~$300k of
  equity - is a 23-hour number. The exitable-at-all-times ceiling is the 21:00 one.
