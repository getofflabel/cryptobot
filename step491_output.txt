HONEST LIMITS OF EVERY NUMBER ABOVE - read before quoting any of them
========================================================================================================
1. 'BANKED' IS A MARK, NOT MONEY. For a position still open at h it is
   the close of bar h. Those rows are not closed and many of them go on
   to stop; the column says what the book is WORTH at h, never what it
   has collected. Only the 'settled' column is realised.
2. THE CAP-RUNNER TABLE IS CONDITIONAL ON SURVIVAL. A cap-runner is
   DEFINED as a position that never touched its stop in 24 hours, so of
   course it is in the green at hour 1 (94.7% of them). That is
   selection, not a forecast: at hour 1 nothing on the tape says which
   of the 29,321 positions still open will be one of them.
3. THE TWO PROFILES DISAGREE AND THE DISAGREEMENT IS THE FINDING. The
   pooled book looks front-loaded because the LOSERS finish early -
   54.1% of all stop-loss is realised inside hour 1 - while the winners
   are still marked and still climbing. The engine that produces the
   entire gross accrues it roughly evenly across all 24 hours.
4. NO EXIT IS IMPLIED ANYWHERE. 'x% of the money has arrived by the
   boundary' is a statement about WHEN, not an argument for closing
   there. Closing at a boundary is a different population with
   different stops and this round did not and may not simulate it.
5. Per-trade net R is NEGATIVE at every horizon on both cost schedules
   (R486 sourced and Alpaca), t by day -4.1 to -9.3. Nothing in this
   round moves that; R490's verdict stands untouched.
