========================================================================================================
THE ITEM'S PREMISE, MEASURED: HOW FAST DOES THE STOP GROW WITH THE
TRIGGER TIMEFRAME?
========================================================================================================
Queue item 14 reasons from 'the stop scales with the trigger's
timeframe at an elasticity of ~1 and the round trip does not scale
at all'. R488's elasticity of ~1 is the stop against VOLATILITY, on
one frame. Against the FRAME itself it is a different number and it
is measured here.

  log(median stop) = -1.402 + 0.386 * log(trigger minutes)   R2 0.9991
  a 60x coarser trigger buys a 4.87x wider stop, not a 60x one.

frame     median stop%  x the 1m stop  cost/stop  x the 1m cost/stop   tight%
  1m            0.242%           1.00      1.187                1.00    16.3%
  5m            0.469%           1.94      0.450                0.38     5.1%
 15m            0.706%           2.92      0.298                0.25     2.7%
 60m            1.176%           4.87      0.169                0.14     0.9%

  The premise is DIRECTIONALLY right and QUANTITATIVELY wrong:
  cost/stop does fall with the timeframe, but at the rate the stop
  actually grows, which is far slower than the frame does.
