1. THE SCHEDULE, SOURCED - AND THE ITEM'S PREMISE IS WRONG
====================================================================================================
  source      : Coinbase blog 'Perpetual futures have arrived in the U.S.' (2025-07-21), read via Internet Archive snapshot 20251117042013
  verbatim    : "fees as low as 0.02%* per contract"
  verbatim    : "*Trading fees are inclusive of exchange, clearing, and
                 NFA fees. A minimum of $0.15 is charged per contract to
                 cover these fixed costs."

  THE ITEM ASSUMED : account cost = CDE $0.10/side  +  CFM % of notional
  WHAT IT ACTUALLY : account cost = max( rate x notional , $0.15 ) per side,
                     with CDE's $0.10 INSIDE it.

  sourced floor rate      : 0.02% per side  (an 'as low as' FLOOR)
  sourced minimum         : $0.15 per contract per side
  inclusive of exch/clr/NFA: True
  standing tier ladder    : UNSOURCED - no value invented for it

  Where the $0.15 minimum binds, CFM's own take over the exchange fee it
  contains is exactly $0.05 per contract per side. That is the number the
  item was trying to bound, and it is a nickel, not a percentage.

  0.02% floor rate is IRRELEVANT - the account pays $0.15 whatever the rate
  says, and would pay $0.15 at a rate of zero.

    SOL: the true increment is +0.0184% RT  ->  OVERSTATED by the item
