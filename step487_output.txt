VERDICT
====================================================================================================
1. EVERY ratio-of-means cost figure in this log is UNDERSTATED, never overstated.
   On crypto the factor is 2.94x-3.19x; on the index it is 1.64x. It is a property of the
   STOP DISTRIBUTION, not of the cost model, so it is the same multiplier on every
   row of a given population - which is why it was invisible: it never changed the
   RANKING of two venues, only the level of all of them at once.

2. NO PER-TRADE FIGURE IN THIS LOG IS WRONG. All five per-trade claims reproduce
   exactly (R485's -0.346/t -3.18 and R474's item-0 foursome, both to 3 decimals).

3. THE SIGN FLIPS ON THE CRYPTO FAMILY AND ONLY THERE. Every crypto cost model
   reads positive net R as a ratio-of-means and negative per trade. On the index at
   0.04% the flip is +0.402 -> -0.024 (t -1.23, not separable from zero either way);
   at 0.02% both statistics are positive.
